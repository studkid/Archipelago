# Chunithm

## What Does Randomization do to this Game?
- You will be given a number of starting songs. The number of which depends on your options.
- Completing any song will give you 2 rewards.
- The items you can recieve are Map Progression and Songs.  You may also recieve duplicate songs (depending on settings) or nothing as filler.

## What is the Goal of Chunithm
The goal of Chunithm is to collect a specified number of **Map Progression**.  Once you'vecollected enough Map Progression, the goal song will be unlocked.

## What is Required to Play the Game in Archipelago?
Access to a Chunithm cabinet, any version should work, just make sure to exclude songs from later versions and select your region correctly.
